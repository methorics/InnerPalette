Inner Palette — Color of Feeling
A metaphorical language for emotions through color. Not a measurement, not a diagnosis.
Instead of asking "What do you feel?" — which the mind often answers with a lie — we ask:
"What color does this feel like in your body? Not the beautiful color. The honest one."
How it works
One color = one state. Color is a metaphor for mixing 4 inner rivers:
C — Threat (metaphor for Cortisol + Adrenaline)
M — Belonging (metaphor for Oxytocin)
Y — Seeking (metaphor for Dopamine)
K — Depth (metaphor for Serotonin + Melatonin)
When one river rises, another falls — this is the waterfall.
Example: #F52A2A → C 0% M 83% Y 83% K 4% → Furious Passion. In this color, M + Y suppress C to 0.
How to use
Download InnerPalette.txt
Paste its full text into a new chat with any AI
AI will show description, copyright, instruction and invitation in your language
Pick a color that responds in the body — Google Color Picker
Send the HEX, e.g. #070f63
No link, no hosting. The file is the prompt. To update, download the new version of the same file.
Important
Hormones here are a METAPHOR. Percentages are percentages of PAINT (CMYK), not of blood.
No hormones are measured. This is not a diagnosis, not medicine, not a hormone test.
Value is in the act of choosing a color and in the conversation after it.
